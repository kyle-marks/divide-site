Common questions

How do I join a group? Ask a member for an invite code, then choose Join with a Code in the app.

How do I change my display name? Open You → Display name (or Account → Display name if you are not in a group yet).

How do I delete my account? Open You → Delete account (or Account → Delete account if you are not in a group yet) and confirm with your Apple ID. Expenses you shared stay in your groups’ records without your name. See the privacy policy for details. If you cannot use the app, email us to request deletion.
