Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Divide is an app for splitting shared expenses within small groups of people who know each other. This policy explains what information Divide handles, why, and what you can do about it.

Divide is operated by Kyle Marks, Vancouver, Canada (“we”). Contact: kyle.m.marks@icloud.com.

What we collect

Your account. You sign in with Apple. We receive an identifier from Apple that is unique to Divide, and the name you choose to share on Apple’s sign-in sheet, which becomes your display name. Divide does not ask Apple for your email address. You can change your display name at any time in the app.

What you and your groups enter. Group names, the members of each group, expenses (amount, description, category, date, who paid and how it was split), recurring expenses, settle-up payments, custom categories, and invite codes.

Device storage. To keep working offline, the app keeps a copy of recently loaded group data on your device. It is removed when you sign out or delete your account.

Service logs. Our hosting provider keeps standard request logs (such as IP address and time of request) to operate and secure the service.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, health, browsing history, or device advertising identifiers.

How we use it

Only to provide Divide: to sign you in, show your groups their shared expenses and balances, and keep the service running and secure. We do not sell your information, show ads, or use it to track you across other apps or websites. Divide contains no third-party analytics or advertising code.

Who can see it

Members of your groups see what is in that group, including your display name and the expenses and payments recorded there. Anyone with a valid invite code can join the group it belongs to, so share invite codes only with people you trust.

Service providers that run Divide on our behalf:

  • Supabase, Inc. hosts the database and sign-in service, in Canada (Central).
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple.

We disclose information otherwise only if required by law.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your information is kept while your account exists.

To delete your account: in the app, open You → Delete account (or Account → Delete account if you are not in a group yet) and confirm with your Apple ID. This immediately:

  • deletes your account and sign-in,
  • disconnects Divide from your Apple ID (Apple’s revocation), and
  • removes your display name.

Expenses and payments you shared with a group stay in that group’s records without your name, because removing them would change the other members’ balances. Encrypted backups are kept for up to 35 days and then deleted.

If you cannot use the app, email kyle.m.marks@icloud.com to request deletion.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to each group’s data is limited to its members by server-side rules.

Children

Divide is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your information. You can correct your display name and delete your account in the app. For anything else, email kyle.m.marks@icloud.com.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app.
