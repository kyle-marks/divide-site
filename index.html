Divide

Split shared expenses, without the spreadsheet.

Divide keeps track of who paid for what in your household, trip, or friend group, and shows everyone what they owe.

What it does

  • Record expenses and split them evenly, by exact amounts, or by percentage.
  • Set up recurring expenses like rent and utilities once.
  • See running balances and record payments when you settle up.
  • Look back at spending by category and month.
  • Keep working offline with your recent group data.

Privacy

You sign in with Apple. Divide has no ads, no tracking, and no third-party analytics. Only the members of a group can see what is in it. Read the privacy policy.

Divide is for iPhone and iPad.
