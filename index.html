Add it before you forget.

Type the amount and go. Who paid and the split are already filled in, and the sheet stays open for the next one, so a pile of receipts takes a minute. Or just tell Siri.

Rent and bills, set once.

Add rent, Hydro, internet or insurance as monthly expenses and they post themselves on the right day, split the way you set them.

See where the month went.

Spending shows up as blocks sized by category. Tap one for its total, how it compares with last month, and every expense in it, then swipe to the next.

Made for iPhone Duo.

Divide changes shape with the phone.

Closed
Add sits in the rail beside your thumb.
Open
The register slides in beside the tab, so you can add while you look things over.
Half-folded
Insights stands up on top, with the controls in reach below.

On iPad, too.

The same tab, laid out for a bigger screen: where you stand and this month's categories on one side, the latest expenses on the other. It works in any orientation and in Split View, and with a keyboard, ⌘N adds an expense.

What Divide leaves out.

Ads and daily limits
No ads, no cap on how many expenses you add, and no waiting between them.
Reminders to pay up
Divide never nudges anyone. The balance just carries over, and you settle when it suits you both.
Your bank
Divide doesn't move money or connect to your bank. Pay each other however you already do, then record it.
Tracking
No analytics, and nothing shared with data brokers. Only the people in a group can see what's in it.
Passwords and email
You sign in with Apple, and Divide never asks for your email address.
