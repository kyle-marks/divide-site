Built for the everyday, not the big trip.

    Type the amount and go. Who paid and the split are filled in, and the sheet stays open for the next one, so a whole receipt pile takes a minute.

What Divide leaves out.

Reminders to pay up
Divide never nudges anyone. The balance just carries over, and you settle when it suits you both.
Your money
Divide doesn't move money or connect to your bank. Pay each other however you already do, then record it.
Ads and tracking
No ads, no analytics, and nothing shared with data brokers. Only the people in a group can see what's in it.
Passwords
You sign in with Apple, and Divide never asks for your email address.
